## Categorization & policy fields

- **App category:** Business  (alt: Jobs is not a Play category; Business fits)
- **Tags:** jobs, hiring, careers, recruitment, Cambodia
- **Contact email:** your support address
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://kramajob.com/privacy
- **Content rating:** complete the questionnaire → expected **Everyone** (no objectionable content; it's a jobs app with user messaging — declare "Users can interact / share content" for the messaging + forum).
- **Data safety:** declare what the site collects via the account — typically: Name, Email, Phone, (optional) résumé/CV files, and app activity. Data is used for account + job-matching; specify whether it's shared (employers see applications the user submits). Mark data encrypted in transit; provide the account-deletion path.
- **Ads:** No (unless you add them).
- **Target audience:** 18+ (working age).
